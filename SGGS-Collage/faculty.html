Our Faculty

SGGS Khalsa College, Mahilpur takes pride in its highly qualified, experienced, and dedicated faculty members who form the backbone of the institution’s academic excellence. The faculty comprises scholars, researchers, and educators who have consistently contributed to teaching, research, and academic development at university, state, and national levels.

With strong academic credentials and a student-centric approach, our faculty members strive to nurture intellectual growth, critical thinking, and ethical values among students. Their commitment to quality education ensures a supportive learning environment and prepares students for professional success and higher studies.
